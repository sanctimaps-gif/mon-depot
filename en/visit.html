🚇 Getting here

The bar is right in the centre of Rouen, in the Vieux-Marché area: everything is walkable from the city-centre stops.

  • Metro: Palais de Justice or Théâtre des Arts
  • TEOR T1, T2, T3: Théâtre des Arts
  • On foot: a few minutes from the Gros-Horloge

Distances are indicative; confirm them according to your starting point.

🅿️ Parking

The centre is largely pedestrianised: it is best to aim for an underground car park and finish on foot.

  • Palais de Justice — the largest in the centre
  • Vieux-Marché — right by the square
  • Park and ride on the outskirts, with a transport ticket

Car park rates and hours should be checked with the operators.
