The place

A friendly bar in central Rouen, a step away from the Vieux-Marché

Le P’tit Ravisé is, above all, a counter: the one where you drink your coffee standing up before work, where you buy your cigarettes and your lottery ticket, where you chat with the owner without needing to order anything at all.

Rue des Bons-Enfants is one of the oldest streets in central Rouen, a few steps from rue Jeanne d'Arc, the Gros-Horloge and place du Vieux-Marché. A bar with a terrace, then, in the Vieux-Marché – Cathedral quarter: a table in the sun the moment it appears, and the shade of the awning the rest of the time.

On foot it is a few minutes from either the Palais de Justice or the Théâtre des Arts metro stops.
